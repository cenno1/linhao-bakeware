Who should contact LINHAO?

What is LINHAO BAKEWARE?

It is the customer-facing B2B bakeware business line of Cixi Linhao Metal Product Co., Ltd. The service covers product selection, OEM/ODM development, coating and color planning, branding and private-label packaging.

Which procurement teams are a good fit?

Brands building a product line, retailers planning an assortment, distributors expanding a category and project buyers that can provide a product reference, estimated quantity, target market and timing.

How should capacity be evaluated?

Use the specific product brief. Pan geometry, material and thickness, existing or new tooling, coating, packaging and quantity all affect the available production plan and lead time.

What is not claimed universally?

Catalog references to tests, standards or social-compliance systems are not presented as company-wide certificates. Scope and supporting documents must be confirmed for the selected product, factory, coating and order.
