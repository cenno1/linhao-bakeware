Testing Standards

Define the method and acceptance rule—not only the test name.

Every inspection or performance claim needs a product-specific method, sample size and acceptance criterion.

Dimensions & workmanship

Length, width, depth, cup geometry, flatness, closure fit, coating coverage, scratches, dents, deformation and logo position.

Functional checks

Project-specific release, thermal-cycle, warping, latch or leakage evaluations under an agreed method and use condition.

Coating & food contact

Applicable adhesion, abrasion or food-contact documentation must match the selected substrate, coating, color, market and order.

Inspection level, sample size, defect classification and acceptance criteria must be written into the purchase specification. Regulatory references are planning sources, not universal LINHAO certifications.

Packaging Cases

Packaging options tied to real buyer decisions

Paper sleeve

Efficient product visibility for single pans or compact sets. Confirm paper grade, closure, barcode position and abrasion protection.

Color box

Full retail communication and stronger shelf presentation. Confirm dimensions, inserts, print finish and transit protection.

Nested bakeware set

Confirm nesting clearance, separators, scratch control, removal sequence, carton dimensions and product-to-pack fit.

Logo and labels

Embossing, printing, labels, inserts and care copy must match the approved product and artwork revision.

Master carton

Confirm pack quantity, dimensions, gross/net weight, marks, orientation and any agreed carton or transit checks.

Packaging mockup

Approve a physical fit sample before mass printing; a digital artwork approval alone does not confirm protection or pack-out.

Procurement Risks

Common risks and how buyers reduce them

Unclear dimensions

Use an approved drawing with measurement points, tolerances and product weight or gauge targets.

Wrong-market documentation

State the destination country and requested evidence before sampling—not after production.

Sample-to-production drift

Keep a golden sample and connect it to the drawing, coating, color, artwork and inspection checklist.

Unverified certification claims

Request documents matching the exact product, coating, factory and order; do not rely on a catalog logo or unrelated report.

Packaging damage

Approve physical pack fit, protection, nesting and master-carton pack-out before mass printing.

Hidden schedule changes

Agree milestone owners and identify which buyer approvals are required before each stage can begin.
