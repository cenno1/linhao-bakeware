Quality Checklist

Confirm what will be inspected.

Dimensional checks

Length, width, height, wall profile, cavity geometry, flatness and latch fit where applicable.

Appearance checks

Color consistency, coating coverage, scratches, dust, dents, deformation and logo position.

Packaging checks

Barcode, artwork, label position, inner protection, carton marks and packing quantity.

Product catalogs mention standards and systems including ISO, BSCI, LFGB, DGCCRF, FDA-related testing and PFOA-free coating claims. These cannot be treated as universal LINHAO certificates. Availability and scope must be confirmed for the chosen product, factory, coating and order.
Authoritative References

Start compliance planning from official sources.

These links provide regulatory context. They are not proof that every LINHAO product or coating is certified for every market.

European Union food-contact framework

Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 on EUR-Lex covers materials and articles intended to come into contact with food. Product-specific applicability and supporting documents must be reviewed for the destination market.

European Union good manufacturing practice

Commission Regulation (EC) No 2023/2006 on EUR-Lex addresses good manufacturing practice for food-contact materials and articles.

United States coating reference

21 CFR 175.300 on eCFR is a U.S. regulatory reference for resinous and polymeric coatings. The selected coating system and intended conditions of use require project-specific review.

Ask for documents that match the exact product, substrate, coating, color, production route and order. A regulation link, catalog statement or unrelated report is not a substitute for project-specific evidence.
