Process and documentation review

Material, construction, coating, packaging and available test documentation are reviewed against the approved specification before shipment.

01 Incoming checkMaterial identity, gauge or target weight and project documents are checked against the order specification.
02 In-process checkDimensions, forming, rim structure, base condition and assembly details are monitored during production.
03 Surface checkFinish appearance, color consistency, coverage and agreed performance tests are reviewed for the selected coating system.
04 Packing checkLabels, inserts, retail packs, carton marks, quantity and shipment pack-out are checked before release.
Inspection scope, sampling level, test methods and third-party testing must be agreed in the purchase specification. Reports are supplied only when applicable to the selected product and confirmed project.
Buyer Questions

Common sourcing questions

What information is needed for a quotation?

Product dimensions, material or target weight, thickness, coating, color, logo method, packaging, expected quantity and destination market.

Can I start from a reference sample or competitor size?

Yes. A reference sample, drawing, target dimensions or clear product photos can be used for initial evaluation. Final feasibility is confirmed through engineering review.

Is there a fixed MOQ?

MOQ depends on mold status, coating color, packaging method and production setup. It is quoted project by project rather than displayed as a universal online number.
