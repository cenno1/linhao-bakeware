Quotation Checklist

Include these six items when possible.

1. Product reference

Catalog page, photo, link, drawing or sample dimensions.

2. Construction

Material, thickness, size, depth, cavity and special structure.

3. Surface

Coating type, internal/external color and appearance target.

4. Branding

Logo method, label, sleeve, insert, box and carton marks.

5. Quantity

Trial quantity, annual forecast or planned order quantity.

6. Market

Destination country and required product documentation.
