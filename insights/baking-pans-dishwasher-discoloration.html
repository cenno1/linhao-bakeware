Why Baking Pans Discolor in the Dishwasher

By LINHAO BAKEWARE · November 19, 2026

A baking pan can leave the dishwasher looking very different from when it went in. Identify the substrate and finish first, then follow the care instructions for that exact product.

Why does an aluminum baking pan turn gray?

Nordic Ware advises hand washing its uncoated Naturals aluminum pans. Its care guidance attributes dishwasher discoloration to heat and cleaning agents and describes that discoloration on this range as cosmetic. That advice applies to the identified product range. For a coated pan, check the coating supplier's and pan manufacturer's instructions before choosing a cleaning method. Nordic Ware aluminum bakeware care

What the recurring questions reveal

A September 2026 Reddit discussion about two cake pans put through a dishwasher drew hundreds of votes. Replies disagreed about the condition of the pans and how to clean them. A separate discussion about milky-looking sheet trays asked whether replacement was necessary. These conversations show how easily buyers can confuse a change in appearance with a change in coating or construction. Photos and anonymous comments alone cannot establish the condition of a particular pan. Cake-pan dishwasher discussion · Sheet-tray appearance discussion

Identify the pan before choosing a cleaner

  • Uncoated aluminum — Confirm that the surface is bare aluminum and look up the model's care instructions.
  • Coated metal — Identify the coating and approved washing method. Abrasive cleaning advice for an uncoated pan may damage a finish.
  • Rolled rims and assembled parts — Check the manufacturer's drying and storage directions, including any parts where water may remain.
  • Unknown construction — Send the seller the model number and clear photographs before attempting aggressive cleaning.

How a retail buyer can evaluate a care claim

Before printing “dishwasher safe,” ask the supplier to identify the complete construction and provide the basis for the claim on the selected item. Record the intended wash program, detergent, cycle count and drying conditions in an agreed test plan. Specify how the sample will be checked afterward, including visible finish changes, coating condition, rim condition and food release.

Keep an unused reference sample for comparison. Photograph the same areas under the same light after each agreed inspection point. Separate observations from the acceptance limits that your team and supplier have approved. The plan should state who reviews an unexpected change and whether a revised coating or care instruction requires another trial.

Write care instructions buyers can find

Include the approved washing method on the retail pack and the product page. Ask whether a permanent marking is practical for the selected pan. Give clear drying, storage and utensil instructions appropriate to its finish. A customer who has discarded the box should still be able to locate the correct guidance using a product name or model number.

Questions to include in a bulk inquiry

Send the required sheet dimensions, substrate, finish, order quantity and target market. Include the planned care statement and describe the evidence you need before approving it. If your retail range combines several constructions, request care guidance for each item.
