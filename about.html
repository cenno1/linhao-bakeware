About LINHAO BAKEWARE

A B2B bakeware partner focused on product fit, communication and execution.

LINHAO BAKEWARE is the customer-facing bakeware business line of Cixi Linhao Metal Product Co., Ltd., serving overseas brands, retailers, distributors and project buyers.

Our Role

Match the right product and production program to the buyer's project.

Rather than displaying retail prices, the website is designed around specification-based B2B inquiries. Each quotation is built from product dimensions, material, thickness, coating, customization, packaging and quantity.

  • ✓
    Product selection
    Organize suitable existing molds and product families from a broad bakeware range.
  • ✓
    Custom development
    Coordinate drawings, sampling, color, logo, packaging and new-mold evaluation.
  • ✓
    Commercial coordination
    Quotation, order follow-up, packaging details and shipping information for overseas buyers.
