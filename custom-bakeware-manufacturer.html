Buyer questions

Custom bakeware manufacturer FAQ.

Can we start with an existing bakeware mold?

Yes. Existing product families can be reviewed against your target specification. If they do not fit the product brief, new tooling can be evaluated from a drawing, reference sample or dimensions.

What can be customized beyond the product itself?

A private-label project can include logo application, labels, paper sleeves, inserts, gift boxes, master cartons and shipping marks, subject to project requirements.

How is a quotation prepared?

Quotations are based on the project specification, including material, thickness, finish, dimensions, packaging and order quantity. No retail pricing is displayed online.
