How to Choose Muffin Pan Cup Count for Retail

Cup count affects customer use, price position, packaging and the role each pan plays in a retail bakeware range.

6-cup pans

A compact choice for smaller households, limited storage and entry-level assortments. It can complement a larger core pan without duplicating the same use case.

12-cup pans

Often a practical everyday format for a core retail range. Confirm cup geometry, overall footprint and liner compatibility against the intended market.

24- and 48-cup pans

Higher cup counts can support mini-bake or entertaining use cases. Review rigidity, handling, product protection and carton efficiency early in the project.

Build a clear assortment

Use each cup count for a distinct customer role. Avoid overlapping formats unless the range has a clear channel or price-position reason.

Buyer checklist: state the target market, cup count, cup size reference, material, coating, packaging level, quantity and target timing.
