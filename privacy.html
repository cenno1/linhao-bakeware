Privacy Policy

When you submit the inquiry form, Web3Forms processes the details you enter and forwards the inquiry to our team for review and a reply. You can also email info@lh-industrial.com directly.

Information you may provide

Name, company, email, country, product requirement, estimated quantity, timing and project messages. Do not include confidential designs or sensitive personal information in an initial inquiry.

How information is used

Information you send by email or WhatsApp is used to evaluate product requirements, prepare quotations, communicate about sampling or orders and maintain necessary business records.

Service providers and retention

Hosting and analytics providers may process ordinary website usage data. Email and WhatsApp messages are handled by the communication services you choose. Business correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed for inquiry follow-up, records, legal obligations and security.

Visit source information

When a referring page identifies a supported AI service, we retain the service name in this browser tab for up to 30 minutes to connect the visit with contact actions and a submitted inquiry. This record contains the service name and expiry time. A new external source clears the previous record. You may optionally tell us how you found LINHAO in the inquiry form. Source information is included with the inquiry and website analytics.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of inquiry information, subject to applicable business and legal record requirements.
